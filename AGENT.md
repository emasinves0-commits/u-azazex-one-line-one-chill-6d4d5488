Entry title: Forty Thousand Left Shoes

> The tide didn't bring in driftwood this morning; it unloaded forty thousand left shoes, all still warm at the heels.

Why it wins:
- Fresh angle: the sea as a courier, not the baby monitors, photos of the sleeper, teeth or voicemails that flood the field.
- Concrete number: forty thousand left shoes is countable and touchable.
- Earned dread: the horror is inferred from the warmth, never announced.
- The last word, "heels", lands the blow.
